# Q439 literature and target check

**27 November 2026. High confidence on source and model, moderate confidence on current literature coverage.** A bounded search found no earlier solution to the exact deployment-time adaptive query problem with unrestricted real linear measurements. The candidate gives an effective exact query-count algorithm and an optimal semialgebraic policy, plus an unbounded separation from a fixed dataset. Its quantifier-elimination construction gives no efficient preprocessing or execution bound.

- Ye, Amin and Özdağlar, *Learning Decision-Sufficient Representations for Linear Optimization*, PMLR 336, 6924–6975, explicitly ask the adaptive question in §7, on paper-numbered p. 13 and proceedings p. 6936, under “Adaptive versus non-adaptive decision information.” This paragraph is in the official proceedings PDF. The versioned arXiv v2 concluding remarks do not include it. Definition 6 gives the common-optimizer condition on a fiber. Their Algorithm 1 adaptively constructs a fixed pointwise-sufficient dataset for one cost. Algorithm 2 constructs a dataset deployed nonadaptively. Neither result determines the minimum worst-case depth of answer-dependent deployment. The pinned Q439 entry imposes a bounded rational standard-form feasible polytope and a nonempty ambient-open convex rational H-polyhedral prior. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/ye26a.html) · [Official PDF, p. 13](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v336/main/assets/ye26a/ye26a.pdf#page=13) · [Author arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18551v2#S8) · [Pinned Q439](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/mathematics/optimization-operations-research.md#q439)
- Bennouna, Bennouna, Amin and Özdağlar, *What Data Enables Optimal Decisions?*, 2025, §§3–4 and Corollary 1, identify the least size of a **fixed** globally sufficient dataset with the dimension of the span of differences of reachable optimal vertices for open convex priors. The 2026 working paper retains the data-informativeness framework. The candidate proves that universal pointwise sufficiency for one fixed measurement set already implies full argmin recovery on an ambient-open prior. This establishes a like-for-like fixed benchmark. [2025 primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21692#S3) · [2026 working paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15365)
- Ye and Bennouna, *Solving Linear and Integer Programs Faster Using Data*, latest arXiv v3 (16 November 2026), give exact affine reformulation from a sufficient subspace and statistical guarantees from samples. The learned subspace is used at deployment as a fixed representation. This does not answer the exact worst-case adaptive query problem. The closely titled *Finite-Resolution Decision Sufficiency for Linear Optimization* describes fixed designs, regret tolerance and bounded noise. Its OpenReview submission detail was unavailable in the public source check, so no theorem numbers from it are used here. [Ye–Bennouna, §§1 and 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25635v3#S3) · [Finite-resolution submission](https://openreview.net/forum?id=FWtqToBwOZ)
- Real quantifier elimination and cylindrical cell decomposition are established tools. Collins provides a general effective method for real closed fields. The contribution is the history recursion and the decision-sufficient examples, with no priority claim for the algebraic tools. Classical nonadaptivity results for linear approximation concern recovery of a fixed linear target over balanced convex classes. An LP optimal decision is a nonlinear correspondence, and those results do not classify this model. [Collins, LNCS 33, 134–183](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/3-540-07407-4_17) · [Traub, Wasilkowski and Woźniakowski, adaptive-information comparison](https://doi.org/10.1109/CDC.1984.272339)

The cube construction gives worst-case depth exactly two against a fixed cost dimension of m for every m ≥ 2, so the savings are strict for m ≥ 3. The supplemental bounded rational box fits the original polyhedral scope. The bounded ellipsoid and extension of the recurrence to rational semialgebraic priors go beyond that scope and are labeled separately. No priority claim is made. Randomized, approximate, noisy, finite-precision and restricted-query models are outside the result.
